# Story: Faction Trust and Character Arcs

## Status

Accepted

## User Story

As a player, I want factions and major characters to respond to my conduct, so that trust, loyalty, coercion, and betrayal shape the crisis and endings.

## Background

The MVP feature requires Mechanical, Stability Office, Keepers, and Descenders at MVP depth, plus core cast members needed for faction, evidence, and route consequences. The requirement calls for prior support from factions and key NPCs to visibly alter crisis handling or consequences.

## Scope

- Define MVP trust or relationship tracking for Mechanical, Stability Office, Keepers, and Descenders.
- Define MVP character states for the core cast needed by the complete-game MVP.
- Ensure faction and character responses can change through cooperation, concealment, challenge, escalation, evidence sharing, repair work, and route decisions.
- Present Voss and the Stability Office with a continuity argument while still exposing abuse, manipulation, disappearance, coercion, and information control.
- Ensure faction and character states influence crisis handling, route context, or ending variants.

## Out of Scope

- Full relationship simulation.
- Romance systems.
- Fully voiced or cinematic character presentation.
- Complete optional dialogue trees for every NPC.
- Final balancing of all trust values.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given the player interacts with a major faction, when they cooperate, conceal, challenge, expose, or escalate, then faction trust or attitude can change at MVP depth.
- Given a major character is affected by player choices, when later scenes reference that character, then their availability, attitude, risk, or outcome reflects prior state.
- Given the player reaches the civic crisis, when faction support or opposition is evaluated, then Mechanical, Stability, Keepers, and Descenders can visibly alter the crisis context or route consequences.
- Given Voss appears in the MVP, when his argument is presented, then the story communicates the continuity rationale without absolving coercion or information control.
- Given the player completes the MVP through different choices, when endings are compared, then at least some character or faction outcomes differ.

## Implementation Notes

Sequence: sixth. This story depends on state tracking and should coordinate with route and ending evaluation.

## Validation

Validate by reviewing at least one consequence chain for each major faction and at least four core character states that can affect later scenes or endings.
