# Story: Vault Zones and Access Progression

## Status

Accepted

## User Story

As a player, I want to move through Vault-9's major zones through believable access progression, so that exploration reinforces Tomas's practical role and the sealed-society premise.

## Background

The complete-game MVP must include Upper Ring, Mid Commons, Lower Mechanica, Black Levels, and Boundary Ring at MVP depth. Access should come through maintenance competence, relationships, evidence, choices, or system failures.

## Scope

- Represent all five major zones at MVP depth.
- Define the minimum locations in each zone needed for the complete-game MVP.
- Define access reasons and progression gates for restricted or dangerous areas.
- Tie zone progression to maintenance tasks, evidence, relationships, pressure, or system failures.
- Ensure each zone contributes to investigation, faction context, evidence, route pressure, or ending setup.

## Out of Scope

- All 20 raw-note locations at full depth if not needed for MVP playthrough coherence.
- Final map UI.
- Procedural exploration.
- Combat-first traversal.
- Full ambient text coverage for every optional location.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given the MVP includes five major zones, when a player completes the playthrough, then each zone appears at MVP depth and contributes to the story.
- Given Tomas accesses a restricted zone, when access is granted, forced, or bypassed, then the reason is grounded in repairs, relationships, evidence, choices, or system failures.
- Given the player reaches the Black Levels, when the scene resolves, then the experience reveals or deepens a contradiction in public doctrine.
- Given the player reaches the Boundary Ring, when the scene resolves, then the experience contributes to exterior-feed, signal, exile, or world-beyond-the-habitat evidence.
- Given a zone is optional or partially optional, when the player skips it, then the MVP still preserves a complete playthrough while reflecting missed evidence, trust, or route context where relevant.

## Implementation Notes

Sequence: fifth. This story depends on the playthrough spine and state behavior. It should inform later design for traversal, scene grouping, and content structure.

## Validation

Validate by mapping the main playthrough to the five zones and checking that each access step has an in-world justification.
